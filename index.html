Reading this: bars show where each market's speculative (non-commercial) net position sits versus its own trailing 3-year range — not versus other markets. A bar pinned to either edge means today's positioning is more crowded than roughly 90% of the last three years of readings.

Extreme positioning has historically preceded reversals at times, but this is not reliable in isolation and includes no price-momentum filter — a market can stay crowded for a long time in a strong trend. Legacy report: the "commercial" category blends true hedgers with swap dealers, which can mute the signal specifically in commodity markets.
